# Site images

Copied from the final prototype (`reference/prototype`). The components ask for them by file name
(`SiteImage file="..."`), so keep the names when replacing a picture:

- `logo-real.jpg`: the NGO logo
- `ig-post1.jpg`, `ig-post2.jpg`, `ig-post3.jpg`: photos from the NGO Instagram (used on the home,
  in the Instagram image of the panel and in the stories placeholders)
- `tampinhas-pode.jpg`, `tampinhas-nao-pode.jpg`: what can and cannot be collected
- `loja-*.jpg`: Loja Patinhas products

Resolution matters. The three Instagram photos here are only 480 x 640 px (thumbnails), so the site
keeps them small (split hero, limited width pictures) to avoid blur. For full width pictures and for the Instagram image
generated in the panel (1080 px), replace them with the original files: at least 1200 px on the
long side, JPEG at about 80% quality, under 400 KB. Ask the NGO for the originals from the phone
gallery; the Instagram page only serves small copies.

Without a file the site shows a neutral block in its place. The NGO allowed the use of its Instagram
photos; ask for more (10 to 15 rescue and adoption photos) to replace the repeated ones.
